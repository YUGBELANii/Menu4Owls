Store hours and overrides

Schedule uses Asia/Kolkata. Defaults are 6:00 PM–3:00 AM. Overrides always need an expiry, within the next 30 days. For a late opening, set the override start to the scheduled opening and its end to the delayed opening; leave normal hours unchanged.
