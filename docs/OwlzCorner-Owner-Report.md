# OwlzCorner — Owner's Report
**Private business note | November 2026**

## Business
OwlzCorner is a small late-night food business based in Tirora, Maharashtra, operating around **6 PM–3 AM**. Its model has focused on takeaway and local delivery.

## People & responsibilities
The business was started and shaped by me. I handled the concept, website, technology and delivery; my siblings joined the operation and handled cooking.

## Website & technology
**Website:** https://owlzcorner.vercel.app

The site was built and deployed using Vercel, with Supabase supporting data and admin functionality. Features developed over time include:
- Digital menu, categories, product management and search
- Cart, order totals and ₹20 delivery calculation
- WhatsApp-based checkout and order references/tokens
- Order history and manual order confirmation/tracking
- Rewards, reviews and basic analytics

## Daily operations
Orders were handled mainly through WhatsApp/phone, with manual confirmation and coordination. Chai had a minimum order quantity of five. The menu direction excluded pizza and rolls.

## Performance snapshot
These are historical figures, not audited current accounts:
- First 20 days: approximately **₹12,000–₹13,000 revenue** and around **₹5,000 profit** after restocking and vehicle-loan costs.
- On **16 November 2026**, the reported cumulative revenue figure was **₹12,291**, including **₹758 that day** through WhatsApp.

## Current direction
The project has been moving beyond a customer-facing ordering website toward an OwlzCorner management system, adapting product, stock and finance functionality for internal operations.

## Owner's assessment
OwlzCorner demonstrated that a small operation could attract customers with limited starting resources and a self-built digital system. The main operational weaknesses were manual order coordination and the difficulty of running a family business with shared responsibilities. The website and management tools remain valuable assets, even as the business model evolves.
