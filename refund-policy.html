01

Order Confirmation

Orders are initiated through our website and completed through WhatsApp. Generating an order token does not, by itself, mean Owlzz Cafe has accepted or confirmed the order. Please send the WhatsApp message to submit your order.

02

Cancellation

Please contact us on WhatsApp as soon as possible if you need to cancel or change an order. Once preparation has started, cancellation may not be possible.

03

Incorrect, Missing, or Damaged Items

If your order arrives with an incorrect or missing item, or an item is damaged, contact us promptly through WhatsApp with your order token and details so we can review the issue.

04

Refunds

Refunds are considered case by case, including where an order was paid for but could not be fulfilled. Any approved refund will be discussed with the customer directly.

05

Delivery Fee

The website currently lists a ₹20 delivery fee. If an order cannot be fulfilled, the treatment of any delivery fee will be discussed with the customer.

06

Contact

For order issues, cancellations, or refund requests, contact Owlzz Cafe through our official WhatsApp ordering number: +91 93706 93869.
